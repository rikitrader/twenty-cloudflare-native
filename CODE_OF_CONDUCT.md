# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for contributors of
all backgrounds and experience levels.

Be respectful, assume good faith, accept constructive feedback, and focus
technical disagreement on evidence and project outcomes. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and disclosure of another
person's credentials or private information are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct threatens the
community or project security. Report conduct concerns privately to the
repository owner through the verified contact method on their GitHub profile.
